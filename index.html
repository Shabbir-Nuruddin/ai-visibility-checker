AI search visibility check

When buyers ask AI, does your name come up?

Ask AI the questions your buyers ask. See who it recommends, and whether you are on the list.

One per line: brand, website, what they sell, market. Each brand gets 5 questions written for it.

How the check works

  1. Write the questions. Five things a buyer types when they need what you sell, without naming you. Or bring your own.
  2. Ask the AI each one, with web search on when the key allows it, the way a buyer would get an answer today.
  3. Read who it named. Every company in each answer, in order, and where you land if you appear at all.
  4. Leave with a reason to talk. A one-line opener built from the result, ready to send to the brand.
Answers come from Gemini or Claude, depending on the key set up. Results change run to run, so treat one check as a sample, not a ranking. Built by Shabbir
